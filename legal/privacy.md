# Privacy policy

**Product validation phase.** MDARIX is in a product validation phase. It is operated by its founder, an individual, while the company that will operate it is being registered. During this phase MDARIX is offered only as a free trial. Once the company is registered, this policy will be updated with the company's details and we will tell you by email.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how the founder of MDARIX, operating it ("MDARIX", "we", "us"), collects and uses personal data when you visit our website, request a demo or a free trial, or use the MDARIX product investigation platform (the "Service").

## Who we are

MDARIX is operated by its founder (Product and Regulatory Quality: 26+ years in enterprise technology and MedTech / life sciences; director-level responsibility for regulatory quality and post-market surveillance; EU MDR, FDA vigilance, CAPA and recalls), in Hyderabad, India. The company that will operate MDARIX is not yet registered; registration is in progress.

Questions about this policy or your personal data: privacy@mdarix.in.

## Our two roles

- **For our own records** (website visitors, demo and trial requests, user accounts, sign-in and security records) we decide how the data is used. We are the data fiduciary (the controller) of that data.
- **For our customers' content** (quality, complaint, supplier, product and investigation records that a customer uploads or connects) we act only on the customer's instructions. The customer decides how that data is used and we process it for the customer. If your personal data is in a customer's records, please contact that customer first; we will help them answer you.

## What we collect

**When you request a free trial:** your name, job title, work email address, organization name and country, and optionally a phone number. We also ask about your organization so the trial fits it: its size, the devices it makes and their highest class, the markets it sells in, where complaints are handled today, how many complaints it receives, what data it plans to use in the trial and what it would like to evaluate.

**When you request a demo:** your name, work email address and phone number, and any times or topics you give us. To limit repeated requests we keep a one-way, salted hash of your network (IP) address, not the address itself.

**When you use the Service:**

- Account details: name, email address, role and organization.
- Sign-in and security records: sign-in times, the authenticator-app secret for two-factor sign-in (stored encrypted), and the network address and browser recorded with security events.
- An audit trail of actions taken in the Service: who did what, and when. Regulated quality work requires this record.
- Electronic signatures: the signer, the meaning of the signature and the time it was applied.
- Trial feedback, if you choose to answer our questionnaire or rating: your answers, linked to your account and organization.
- A request to end a trial early: the reason you give, if any, and whether you asked for your data to be deleted.

**Customer content** may contain personal data, for example a name in a complaint description. We process it only to provide the Service to the customer. During the trial, customers are asked not to enter patient-identifiable data.

## Why we use it

- To provide the Service, run free trials and answer demo requests, which you asked for.
- To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse and keep an audit trail.
- To send account, security, trial and service emails: activation, password reset, trial reminders and notifications you have set up.
- To improve the Service from trial feedback. We publish a quote from your feedback only if you chose named or anonymous publication; you can withdraw that choice at any time and the quote is removed.
- To meet legal obligations and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## Artificial intelligence features

AI features are off for a new organization until its administrator switches them on, and they stay off for free trials during the product validation phase. When they are on, the text of the records needed for a request is sent to an AI provider we have contracted, masked according to the organization's settings. We use only providers that have agreed not to train their models on that content and to keep it for a limited period, or models we host ourselves. AI output is always labelled as such and is never treated as a verified fact or a decision.

## Who we share it with

We use service providers to run the Service. Each receives only what it needs for its task and is bound by its terms to protect it. Today they are:

- **Google Cloud** (Google Cloud India Private Limited): hosting, database, file storage and encrypted backups, in India.
- **Zoho** (Zoho Corporation Private Limited, India): delivery of the emails we send, such as invitations, password resets and trial reminders (ZeptoMail), and our mailboxes, such as support@mdarix.in and privacy@mdarix.in (Zoho Mail), from Zoho's data centres in India. ZeptoMail receives the recipient's name and email address and the email's content. Quality records are never sent by email.

We will update this list before any other provider receives personal data. We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the public.

## Where it is processed

The Service and its data are hosted in India, by Google Cloud in its Mumbai region, with encrypted backup copies in its Delhi region. Our emails are sent and received through Zoho's data centres in India. We do not transfer personal data outside India.

## How long we keep it

- Free trial workspaces: deleted 30 days after the trial ends. The person who requested the trial is emailed 7 days and 1 day before the trial ends, and when it ends. A trial that is ended early at your request, with deletion asked for, is deleted when we confirm the request.
- Customer accounts and content: kept for the subscription term. At the end, the customer's data is returned as a full export (unless the customer waives this in writing) and then deleted.
- Audit trail and electronic signatures: kept unchanged for at least 7 years from each event, or for as long as the customer's account exists if that is longer. When a customer's data is deleted at the end of its contract, its audit trail is given to the customer in the export and then deleted.
- Backups: encrypted and kept for up to 90 days, after which deleted data no longer exists in them.
- A legal hold (where a court, regulator or the customer requires data to be preserved) pauses deletion for that organization until it is lifted.

When personal data is erased, some traces can remain. Audit records keep an account identifier that no longer identifies the person, together with the network address and browser recorded at the time. Free text that colleagues typed into quality records is not searched for names. Erased data remains in backups until they expire; if a backup is ever restored, the erasures are repeated before the Service is used again.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use one cookie: a strictly necessary session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is secure and cannot be read by scripts. It ends after 30 minutes without activity, and at the latest 12 hours after sign-in.

Your browser's local storage keeps preferences only, such as dismissed notices, table column choices and onboarding progress. We use no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## How we protect it

- Encryption of all traffic (TLS).
- Separation of each organization's data in the database.
- Encrypted authenticator secrets, connector credentials, uploaded files and backups.
- Passwords stored only as salted hashes and checked against known breached passwords.
- Sign-in rate limits and lockout, and two-factor sign-in.
- Virus scanning of uploads.
- A tamper-evident audit trail that is verified daily.

More detail is on our [Security page](/security).

If a personal-data breach affects a customer's content, we tell the customer, and you where the law requires it, without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

If a personal-data breach affects the personal data we control for our own records, we tell each affected person, in plain words and without delay, in their MDARIX account or by email. We tell you what happened, including the nature and extent of the breach and when it happened; the consequences it is likely to have for you; what we have done and are doing to reduce the risk; what you can do to protect yourself; and how to reach a person who can answer your questions. We also tell the Data Protection Board of India without delay, and send it a detailed report within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access your personal data;
- correct and complete it;
- have it erased;
- restrict or object to its use;
- receive it in a portable format;
- withdraw any consent you gave;
- nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to;
- have a grievance about our handling of your data addressed.

To use these rights, email privacy@mdarix.in. So that we can identify you, write from the email address on your MDARIX account, or the one you gave us when you requested a trial or a demo. If you no longer have access to that address, say so in your email and we will verify your identity another way before we act on your request.

Our contact for grievances under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is the founder of MDARIX, at that address. We respond to a grievance within one month of receiving it. If your data is part of a customer's content, we will pass your request to that customer and help them respond.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
