## Our two roles

- **For our own records** (website visitors, demo and trial requests, user accounts, sign-in and security records) we decide how the data is used. We are the controller of that data.
- **For our customers' content** (quality, complaint, supplier, product and investigation records that a customer uploads or connects) we act only on the customer's instructions. The customer is the controller and we are its processor, under our agreement with that customer. If your personal data is in a customer's records, please contact that customer first; we will help them answer you.

## What we collect

**When you request a free trial:** your name, work email address and organization name.

**When you request a demo:** your name, work email address and phone number. To limit repeated requests we keep a one-way, salted hash of your network (IP) address, not the address itself.

**When you use the Service:**

- Account details: name, email address, role and organization.
- Sign-in and security records: sign-in times, the authenticator-app secret if you turn on two-factor sign-in (stored encrypted), and the network address and browser recorded with security events.
- An audit trail of actions taken in the Service: who did what, and when. Regulated quality work requires this record.
- Electronic signatures: the signer, the meaning of the signature and the time it was applied.
- Trial feedback, if you choose to answer our questionnaire: your answers, linked to your account and organization.

**Customer content** may contain personal data, for example a name in a complaint description. We process it only to provide the Service to the customer. During a pilot or trial, customers are asked not to enter patient-identifiable data.

## Artificial intelligence features

AI features are off for a new organization until its administrator switches them on. AI output is always labelled as such and is never treated as a verified fact or a decision.

## How long we keep it

- Free trial workspaces: deleted 30 days after the trial ends. The person who requested the trial is emailed 7 days and 1 day before the trial ends, and when it ends.
- Customer accounts and content: kept for the subscription term. At the end, the customer's data is returned as a full export (unless the customer waives this in writing) and then deleted.
- Audit trail and electronic signatures: kept unchanged for at least 7 years from each event, or for as long as the customer's account exists if that is longer. When a customer's data is deleted at the end of its contract, its audit trail is given to the customer in the export and then deleted.
- Backups: encrypted and kept for up to 90 days, after which deleted data no longer exists in them.
- A legal hold (where a court, regulator or the customer requires data to be preserved) pauses deletion for that organization until it is lifted.

When personal data is erased, some traces can remain. Audit records keep an account identifier that no longer identifies the person, together with the network address and browser recorded at the time. Free text that colleagues typed into quality records is not searched for names. Erased data remains in backups until they expire; if a backup is ever restored, the erasures are repeated before the Service is used again.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use one cookie: a strictly necessary session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is secure and cannot be read by scripts. It ends after 30 minutes without activity, and at the latest 12 hours after sign-in.

Your browser's local storage keeps preferences only, such as dismissed notices, table column choices and onboarding progress. We use no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## How we protect it

- Encryption of all traffic (TLS).
- Separation of each organization's data in the database.
- Encrypted authenticator secrets, connector credentials and backups.
- Passwords stored only as salted hashes and checked against known breached passwords.
- Sign-in rate limits and lockout, and two-factor sign-in.
- Virus scanning of uploads.
- A tamper-evident audit trail that is verified daily.

More detail is on our [Security page](/security).
