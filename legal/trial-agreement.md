This agreement applies when Srinivas Telikicherla, operating MDARIX ("MDARIX", "we", "us"), gives your organization a free trial of the MDARIX product investigation platform (the "Service"). Our [Terms of service](/terms) and [Privacy policy](/privacy) also apply to the trial. Where they conflict with this agreement, this agreement prevails for the trial.

## 1. Requesting a trial

1.1 You can request a trial on our website with your name, job title, work email address, organization name and country, and some answers about your organization.

1.2 We may approve a request at once, for example when it comes from a company email address, or review it first; we may decline a request at our discretion. When a request is approved, we create a private workspace for your organization and email an invitation to the person who made the request.

## 2. What the trial includes

- **Length:** 30 days from the start of the trial.
- **Users:** up to 3 people from your organization.
- **Data:** up to 5,000 uploaded rows.
- **Sample data:** the workspace starts with sample data so you can explore the Service straight away.
- **Price:** the trial is free of charge, and no payment details are needed.

Some features may be limited or unavailable during the trial. AI features are off during the product validation phase.

## 3. Using the trial

3.1 The trial is for evaluating the Service for your organization's own internal use.

3.2 Please use sample data, or data that does not identify patients. Do not enter patient-identifiable data during the trial.

3.3 You are responsible for what your users upload and for their use of the Service, as described in the Terms of service.

3.4 Decisions remain yours. The Service supports your people; it does not replace their judgement or your quality and regulatory processes.

## 4. No commitments during the trial

4.1 The trial is provided "as is", without any warranty or service-level commitment, to the extent the law allows.

4.2 We will try to help with questions at support@mdarix.in, but we do not guarantee response times during a trial.

4.3 To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for any loss arising from the trial, and our total liability for the trial is limited to ten thousand Indian rupees (₹10,000). Nothing in this agreement limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 5. When the trial ends

5.1 We email the person who requested the trial 7 days and 1 day before the trial ends, and again when it ends.

5.2 When the trial ends, access to the workspace stops.

5.3 Your trial workspace and everything in it are deleted 30 days after the trial ends. Deleted data cannot be recovered after that, except that encrypted backups are kept for a limited period, as described in the Privacy policy.

5.4 If your organization goes on as a design partner or subscriber before the workspace is deleted, we will agree with you in writing whether the trial workspace and its data carry over.

5.5 You can ask to end the trial early at any time from the workspace, and ask for your data to be deleted at the same time. We confirm the request, usually within one business day, and email you when the trial has ended. We may also end a trial early; if we do, we will tell you.

## 6. Feedback

6.1 We may ask you to answer a short questionnaire or a one-click rating about the trial. Answering is optional.

6.2 We may use your feedback to improve the Service.

6.3 We publish a quote from your feedback only if you chose to have it published, either with your name or anonymously, and only after we review it. You can withdraw that choice at any time and the quote will be removed.
