# MDARIX 0.10.93: a note for trial owners

**A Case is now the first contact.** When a customer calls, writes or a service engineer reports a problem, log it
with **Log a case** (Complaints). The Case keeps the reporter's own words and the time you first knew. The **Case
check** asks ten questions and suggests whether it is a complaint, a complaint with service work, service only, an
inquiry, pending or not a complaint. A person decides and signs. When you sign a complaint, the Case becomes the
complaint at once: its facts and its awareness time come with it, so no clock starts again. If the complaint is
already in your list, attach the Case to it instead.

**What we called a case is now an investigation.** The screens, the help and the downloaded records say
"investigation" where they said "case": for example **Investigation evidence** and **Investigation timeline**. Your
saved links and bookmarks still open the right place.

**Triage has two speeds.** When harm is possible, the complaint goes on the fast track: a named person acknowledges it
within an hour, or it goes to the next person in line. Everything else gets a routine one-click confirmation.
If your complaints file carries your QMS triage (four optional columns in the template), MDARIX keeps it: a complaint
with no sign of harm is accepted as triaged, one with a sign of harm waits for you to **Confirm QMS triage**. Old
complaints you import with no sign of harm wait in their own **Imported backlog** view. A complaint closed in your QMS
with a death or a serious injury and no reportability decision comes to the **Second look**.

**A new persona, Service Desk.** Invite your service desk colleagues with it: they log and check Cases. Investigators
no longer classify first contacts or triage; Regulatory Reviewers may now triage.

**Getting started, Part 1** now starts from the first contact: open SAMPLE-CASE-11 (the hospital's call behind
SAMPLE-RV-11), answer the one open question, sign it, and follow the complaint to its submission. Each step says what
to do, why, who does it and what counts as done. Your own records and earlier progress are never changed.
