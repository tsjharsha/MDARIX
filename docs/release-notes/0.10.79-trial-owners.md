# MDARIX 0.10.79: a note for trial owners

Since 0.10.73 you choose a persona for each colleague you invite, such as Regulatory Leader, Complaint Handler or
Investigator. The persona decides which screens they see and what they may sign. Colleagues you invited before 0.10.73
did not get a choice, so they keep the Quality Leader persona, with the same rights as before. Nothing changes for them
until you change it.

To change a colleague's persona, open **Team** (on the classic menu: **Getting started › Team**). Next to their name,
choose the new persona from the list, then press **Change the persona** to confirm. The change applies at once. Their
earlier work stays as it was, and only you can change their persona again.

Also new in this release: a Regulatory Leader can now edit **Settings › Products**: each product's UDI-DI, Basic
UDI-DI, your own GMDN code, the EMDN code, its registrations per market and your responsible parties.
