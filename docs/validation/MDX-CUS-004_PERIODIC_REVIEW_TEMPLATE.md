## 1. Purpose and scope

The periodic review shows whether MDARIX, as you use it, is still in the state your UAT/PQ (MDX-CUS-001) accepted,
and records what changed in the period. It covers your organisation [name] in MDARIX for the period [from] to [to].

## 2. Rules

- **PR-R01** The review shall be held every 12 months from your go-live (MDARIX's recommended interval, owner
  decision B6b-D18; MDX-VAL-001 VMP-R73), or more often if your procedure says so.
- **PR-R02** The review shall have one named owner in your organisation (section 3).
- **PR-R03** Each section 4 item shall be answered with its source and a finding; "none in the period" is an answer,
  a blank is not.
- **PR-R04** Every finding that needs action shall have an owner and a due date in section 5.
- **PR-R05** The conclusion shall be one of: "remains fit for our intended use", "fit, with the actions in section
  5", or "not fit", with the reason.
- **PR-R06** The report shall be approved by your QA.
