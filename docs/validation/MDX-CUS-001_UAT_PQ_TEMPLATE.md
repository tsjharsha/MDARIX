**How to use this template.** This is a template for your organisation. Copy the Word file
(`docs/validation/customer-templates/MDX-CUS-001_v0.1.docx`) and the Excel log
(`MDX-CUS-001_v0.1_uat-pq-log.xlsx`), fill in every field in square brackets, and keep the filled copy in your own
document system under your own procedures. Your filled copy is your record, not MDARIX's: MDARIX never stores it in
its repository (MDX-VAL-000 DC-R06). Words in square brackets are for you to replace.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This template is for your user acceptance test and performance qualification (UAT/PQ) of MDARIX: the test that shows
MDARIX supports **your** intended use, with **your** configuration, in the hands of **your** business users.

MDARIX validates each release for MDARIX's own intended use (MDX-VAL-001; MDARIX's own PQ is MDX-VAL-008, which
states it is not a customer's PQ: `docs/validation/MDX-VAL-008_PQ_PROTOCOL.md:28-30`). You validate your use. Your
UAT/PQ does not repeat MDARIX's installation and operational qualification; it relies on the release pack MDARIX
gives you for each release (MDX-CUS-006 section 3).

Out of scope of this template: your supplier assessment of MDARIX (the supplier pack MDX-SUP-001 to -006), your data
migration (MDX-CUS-002 and -003) and your go-live decision (MDX-CUS-005).

## 6. Rules

- **UAT-R01** Each test case shall trace to a row of your intended use (section 2).
- **UAT-R02** Your QA shall approve the expected result of each test case before its first execution.
- **UAT-R03** A test case shall be executed by a business user who performs that task in your organisation.
- **UAT-R04** The person who approves the UAT/PQ shall not have executed any of its test cases.
- **UAT-R05** Each execution shall record the executor, the date (UTC), the actual result, the result and an evidence
  ID.
- **UAT-R06** The result shall be one of PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or NOT RUN.
- **UAT-R07** UAT/PQ shall run in an organisation that holds no live regulated records, unless your QA approves a
  written reason. Audit rows are never updated or deleted (requirement REQ-AUD-001), so test records made in a live
  organisation stay in its audit trail for good.
- **UAT-R08** The organisation used shall carry the configuration named in section 4; any change during execution
  shall be a new signed snapshot and a deviation.
- **UAT-R09** Each test case marked "negative" shall pass only when MDARIX refuses the action and the refusal is
  visible to the user.
- **UAT-R10** A FAIL shall be logged as a deviation (section 9) before anything is changed.
- **UAT-R11** Evidence shall be a screenshot, export or download saved under its evidence ID in your document
  system; each evidence file shall carry the test case ID in its name.

## 8. Execution record

Record each execution in the Excel log (sheet "Test cases"), one row per execution: executed by, date (UTC), actual
result, result, evidence ID and, for a result other than PASS, the deviation ID. A re-execution is a new row; the
earlier row stays.

## 9. Deviations

- **UAT-R12** Every result other than PASS shall be recorded as a deviation under your own deviation procedure (sheet
  "Deviations").
- **UAT-R13** A deviation that may be a defect in MDARIX shall be reported to MDARIX support with the test case ID,
  the steps, the time (UTC) and redacted evidence; MDARIX logs it in its own deviation log (MDX-VAL-010) and states
  the outcome in writing.
- **UAT-R14** A deviation shall be closed by your QA only with a retest result.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

- **UAT-R15** The UAT/PQ shall pass only when every kept test case is PASS, or has a deviation closed with a retest
  PASS.
- **UAT-R16** A BLOCKED or NOT RUN test case shall never count as a pass.
- **UAT-R17** Your QA shall record any test case dropped from section 7, with the reason.
