## 1. Purpose and scope

These instructions tell your QA what MDARIX validates for each release, what you validate, and how to use the
documents MDARIX gives you. They apply to every organisation that uses MDARIX for regulated work, in both operating
modes, MDARIX Connect and MDARIX Complete (`docs/validation/MDX-B10-001-Intended-Use-Statement.md:76-79`).

MDARIX is software as a service. MDARIX installs, hosts and operates it; every customer runs the same version. You
do not install anything, so you do not run an installation qualification of MDARIX.

## 2. Status today

MDARIX's validation plan (MDX-VAL-001) is a draft. Until the summary report (VSR) of the first release pack under
that plan is approved, MDARIX states its validation status only as: "Validation in progress; plan MDX-VAL-001
(draft)." (MDX-VAL-001 VMP-R44, `docs/validation/MDX-VAL-001_VALIDATION_MASTER_PLAN.md:293-294`). No release pack
has been generated yet (`docs/validation/README.md:168`). Ask MDARIX for the current status in writing before you
rely on any release.

## 3. Who validates what

| Party | Validates and maintains |
|---|---|
| MDARIX | Each release, for MDARIX's own intended use: requirements, risk assessment, installation and operational qualification on a controlled environment, MDARIX's own performance qualification on a sample story (MDX-VAL-008), deviations, the summary report and a signed release approval before every production deploy (MDX-VAL-001 sections 9 and 12). Hosting, infrastructure security and patching; backups, quarterly restore drills and recovery validation (MDX-OPS-001); time synchronisation; control of sub-processors; change notices; incident notification |
| You | Your intended use: your UAT/PQ (MDX-CUS-001) with your configuration; your configuration and its signed snapshot; user accounts and roles; access reviews and audit-trail reviews with MDARIX's tools; your e-signature policy and, where you report to FDA, your certification letter under 21 CFR 11.100(c); training records; the accuracy of data you enter or import (MDX-CUS-002, -003); checking each starter rule against your own procedure; submission to authorities (MDARIX prepares report files and never submits them: `docs/validation/MDX-B10-001-Intended-Use-Statement.md:86`); your periodic review (MDX-CUS-004); your outage procedure |
| Both | Data retention and exit; inputs to your periodic review; investigation of a deviation that affects you |

This split is MDARIX's proposal to its consultant (`docs/validation/MDX-VAL-013_CONSULTANT_BRIEF.md:88-94`); it is
final when the consultant has confirmed it.

## 4. Rules

- **CVI-R01** You shall validate your intended use before you use MDARIX for regulated work.
- **CVI-R02** You shall not rely on a release whose release pack you have not received.
- **CVI-R03** You shall assess each change notice and decide, under your change control, whether the change needs
  re-testing of your use.
- **CVI-R04** You shall keep your filled templates, results and approvals in your own document system; MDARIX does
  not keep them (MDX-VAL-000 DC-R06).
- **CVI-R05** You shall give a copy of a filled template to MDARIX only when you choose to, and without personal data.
- **CVI-R06** You shall hold a periodic review every 12 months, or more often if your procedure says so (MDX-CUS-004).

## 6. How to validate your use, step by step

| Step | What you do | Template |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assess MDARIX as a supplier, with the supplier pack | Your supplier procedure |
| 2 | Write your intended use and your risk rating of each process | MDX-CUS-001 section 2 |
| 3 | Set up your configuration; your customer administrator signs its snapshot | MDX-CUS-001 section 4 |
| 4 | Plan and run your data migration, if you have one | MDX-CUS-002, MDX-CUS-003 |
| 5 | Choose and approve your test cases; your business users execute them; your QA approves the summary | MDX-CUS-001 |
| 6 | Go live when the checklist is complete | MDX-CUS-005 |
| 7 | For each release: read the change notice and release pack, assess the change, re-test where your change control says so, and sign a new configuration snapshot if your configuration changed | Your change control; MDX-CUS-001 (re-test) |
| 8 | Every 12 months: periodic review | MDX-CUS-004 |

## 8. What MDARIX never claims

MDARIX does not say it is validated for your use, and it makes no certification claim. MDARIX validates each release
for its own intended use; you validate yours (MDX-VAL-000 DC-R30). Any statement of MDARIX's validation status
follows section 2.
