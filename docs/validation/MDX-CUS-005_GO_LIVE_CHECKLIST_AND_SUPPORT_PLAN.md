## 1. Purpose and scope

This checklist decides whether your organisation goes live on MDARIX, and the support plan says how MDARIX supports
you afterwards. It covers your organisation [name] going live on MDARIX release [version] on [date].

## 2. Rules

- **GL-R01** Go-live shall not happen while an item in section 3 is open, unless your QA accepts it in writing in
  section 5, with a reason and a due date.
- **GL-R02** Each item shall be confirmed by the party named in its "Confirms" column, with initials and date.
- **GL-R03** An item that cannot be met shall never be marked done; it is open or accepted (GL-R01).

### 4.2 Severities

MDARIX classes incidents by these severities (`docs/runbooks/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md:25-33`). When unsure, the higher
severity is chosen.

| Severity | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| SEV1 | Customer data exposed or at risk, data integrity in doubt, or the service is down for everyone | Suspected breach or cross-organisation access, audit-chain failure, the service down, lost data |
| SEV2 | A major function is broken for one or more customers; no data at risk | Worker stopped, e-mail not sending, backups failing, service degraded for over an hour |
| SEV3 | Limited impact or a workaround exists | A few undelivered events, one failing connector |

### 4.3 Response targets

Time to a person acknowledging and starting work, around the clock unless it says business day
(`docs/handover/52_SUPPORT_AND_OWNERSHIP_HANDOVER.md:28-38`). These are MDARIX's internal targets for monitored
signals; the targets for a request you raise are [to be agreed in the contract].

| Signal | Severity | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Audit trail integrity check failed | SEV1 | 1 hour |
| Service down | SEV1 | 1 hour |
| Background worker stopped | SEV2 | 1 hour |
| Backup failed, or newest off-site backup older than 26 hours | SEV2 | Same business day |
| Undelivered (dead-lettered) events | SEV3 | Next business day |

How MDARIX tells you about an incident that affects you: as `docs/runbooks/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md` section 4.1 sets.
Recovery point stated to customers: 24 hours today (daily encrypted backup, no point-in-time recovery); target 15
minutes with point-in-time recovery on pilot hosting (owner decision B6b-D8; `docs/runbooks/BACKUP_AND_RESTORE.md:3-5`).
Recovery time target: 4 hours (`docs/runbooks/BACKUP_AND_RESTORE.md:5`).
