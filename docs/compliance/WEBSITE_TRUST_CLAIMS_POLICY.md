# MDARIX website trust-claims policy

## Approved current wording

MDARIX may describe implemented controls such as tenant-scoped authorization,
role and field policies, audit records, provenance, human approval controls,
and advisory AI safeguards. Demonstrations may be described as synthetic MVP
validation using local or controlled validation infrastructure.

## Prohibited unless separately approved with evidence

- ISO 27001 certification or certification-equivalent wording.
- HIPAA compliance.
- GDPR compliance.
- 21 CFR Part 11 compliance.
- FDA, EU MDR, ISO, or Notified Body approval/certification.
- Customer logos, metrics, testimonials, or security attestations without written approval.

## Required review before publication

Every trust claim must have an owner, source evidence, scope, environment,
review date, and expiry/review trigger. Claims must distinguish implemented,
synthetic/demo-only, planned, and production-dependent behavior.

## Sample dossiers

Only approved, non-confidential, synthetic sample dossiers may be downloadable.
They must carry a visible `SYNTHETIC / DEMONSTRATION ONLY` watermark and a
readme explaining that they are preparation examples, not FDA submissions,
Notified Body technical files, or regulatory advice.

## Current site decision

The Security page explicitly states that MDARIX does not claim ISO 27001,
HIPAA, GDPR, or 21 CFR Part 11 certification/compliance. Production legal,
privacy, security, and validation reviews remain required before making any
additional regulated claim.
