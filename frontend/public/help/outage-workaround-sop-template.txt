MANUAL WORKAROUND WHEN MDARIX IS UNAVAILABLE - STARTER SOP TEMPLATE

Starter template: adapt it to your own procedures. To confirm with your consultant.
It does not tell you how or when to report to an authority: your own reporting SOP does.

Owner (role): ______________________   Approved by (role): ______________________

1. PURPOSE
   Legal reporting clocks keep running when MDARIX is unavailable. This procedure keeps complaint intake,
   reportability decisions and report deadlines under control during an outage, and brings the records back into
   MDARIX completely afterwards.

2. SCOPE
   Complaints, calls, e-mails and other reports of a possible adverse event or malfunction received while MDARIX
   cannot be used (planned maintenance, an outage, a network failure at our site).

3. WHEN TO SWITCH TO MANUAL MODE
   3.1 MDARIX cannot be reached, or cannot save, for more than ____ minutes (we suggest 30), or the MDARIX team
       announces an outage.
   3.2 The person who notices tells: ______________________ (role), who declares manual mode and records the time.
   3.3 Check MDARIX's status page: ______________________ (address from your MDARIX contract or the support team).

4. DEADLINES DURING THE OUTAGE
   4.1 Use the latest daily deadline e-mail from MDARIX ("Open reporting deadlines"), or the latest printed copy
       (Oversight > Deadlines PDF). Keep the printed copy at: ______________________.
   4.2 The person responsible for each deadline is the owner shown on the list; if absent: ______________________.
   4.3 A report due during the outage is prepared and submitted under your own reporting SOP
       (document number: ____________), outside MDARIX.

5. PAPER OR SPREADSHEET LOG (one row per contact; the columns match MDARIX's "Log a case")
   - Log row number
   - Received: date, time and time zone
   - When we became aware (first knowledge): date, time, time zone, who knew (us, a distributor, ...)
   - Channel (phone, e-mail, visit, ...) and its reference
   - Reporter's site and country
   - Product, lot, serial number, UDI (as stated)
   - Event date and time
   - The reporter's own words (verbatim)
   - Patient involved? What happened to them?
   - What was asked for (replacement, investigation, ...)
   - Urgent (death, serious injury, public health threat)? yes / no
   - Entered by (name, signature) and when
   - Back-entered in MDARIX as (Case reference) - filled in after recovery
   Keep personal data of patients and reporters to what your procedures allow.

6. WHO DECIDES REPORTABILITY MEANWHILE
   6.1 Urgent contacts go at once to: ______________________ (role), by phone: ______________________.
   6.2 Reportability decisions made during the outage are recorded on paper with the decider's name, date and
       reasoning, under your own procedure, and entered in MDARIX after recovery.

7. AFTER RECOVERY: BACK-ENTRY
   7.1 Enter every log row in MDARIX with "Log a case". Use the real times from the log: MDARIX counts every clock
       from when you became aware, never from the time of entry, so a back-entered Case may already be due or
       overdue.
   7.2 When you became aware more than 24 hours before the entry, MDARIX asks for a late-entry reason. Write, for
       example: "MDARIX unavailable <date> <from>-<to>; entered from manual log row <n>".
   7.3 Before saving, MDARIX shows records that may be the same event (Cases, complaints, e-mails received
       meanwhile). If it is the same event, add the contact to that record; do not create a second one.
   7.4 Record decisions taken on paper (section 6) on the Case or complaint, with the original date in the reason.
   7.5 Write the MDARIX Case reference on each log row.

8. COMPLETENESS CHECK AFTER RECOVERY
   8.1 ______________________ (role, not the person who entered the rows) checks that every log row has an MDARIX
       reference, and that every deadline of the outage period was met or its delay is documented.
   8.2 Sign and date the log; file it with: ______________________.
   8.3 A missed or late deadline is handled under your deviation / CAPA procedure (document number: ____________).

9. RECORDS
   Manual log (signed), printed deadline list used, completeness check, any deviation raised.
